Reports

Kitna kamaya ya ghata: house profit from all games, bonus cost and cash (India time).

Profit & Loss

Game profit = everything players staked minus everything games paid back. It swings day to day and settles near the house edge as bets add up.
Net profit = game profit minus bonuses given away (gift codes, deposit bonuses, VIP rewards). Admin test credits are shown apart and not counted.
Cash = real deposits in minus withdrawals paid out. Players can still withdraw what is in their wallets, so the last box is what would be left if every player cashed out today.

Har game ka profit / loss

Each game's result from its own bets: what players staked, what it paid back, and the profit. Green = the house is up on that game, red = down. A game needs a few thousand bets before its % settles near its edge; until then one big win can swing it.

Max win per bet

The most any single bet can pay, in every game. Players see it in each game. A lower limit cuts the house's worst single loss, but also caps big wins.
