UPI deposits

UPI deposits through RupayEx (real money), plus past StockPay ones. StockPay is switched off for good: none of its orders can ever be credited again. PENDING means the player hasn't finished paying yet, FAILED means the payment page expired or was cancelled. A deposit still PENDING after 30 minutes is flagged red as STUCK.

Deposit safety

Take back a player's StockPay TEST deposits (play money): each is marked reversed and can never be credited again, whatever StockPay later reports. Use "Reverse" on a single deposit in the list above for anything else. The balance check lists players whose balance differs from what their transactions add up to — a sign the balance was changed by hand.

Reports

Kitna kamaya ya ghata: house profit from all games, bonus cost and cash (India time).

Profit & Loss

Game profit = everything players staked minus everything games paid back. It swings day to day and settles near the house edge as bets add up.
Net profit = game profit minus bonuses given away (gift codes, deposit bonuses, VIP rewards). Admin test credits are shown apart and not counted.
Cash = real deposits in minus withdrawals paid out. Players can still withdraw what is in their wallets, so the last box is what would be left if every player cashed out today.

Har game ka profit / loss

Each game's result from its own bets: what players staked, what it paid back, and the profit. Green = the house is up on that game, red = down. A game needs a few thousand bets before its % settles near its Target % (the edge it is set to keep); until then one big win can swing it.
